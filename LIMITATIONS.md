# Limitations

Regional reanalysis grid, not parcel measurement. Historical reconstruction, not a forecast. The water score is not water saved nor harvest probability. Only water is computed, so the overall score is intentionally unavailable. Soil water parameters come from a SoilGrids regional estimate through a pedotransfer function (or from the farmer, or labelled demo values), not from a measurement of the plot. Catalog covers maize and bean only; orchards are out of scope. No field validation yet. Not production agronomic advice.
