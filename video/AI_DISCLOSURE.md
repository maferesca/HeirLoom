# AI disclosure: Heirloom demo (submission text)

## Space Apps "AI Tools" field (short version)

Claude Code (Anthropic; Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5) assisted with writing and revising the prototype code and with assembling the demo video (screen-capture scripts, Remotion edit, audio). The demo narration is a synthetic voice generated offline with Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, via kokoro-onnx). The music and sound effects were synthesized by a NumPy script written with Claude Code, with no music model or samples. The logo was generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI). The website hero illustration, the contour backgrounds and the video's seed scenes, titles and highlights are code-generated and labelled "AI-generated visuals". The Earth zoom uses real Mapbox satellite imagery (© Mapbox © OpenStreetMap © Maxar) with an AI-scripted camera animation. The app screens are real captures. The climate data come from NASA POWER, and the soil data are a SoilGrids 2.0 (ISRIC) regional estimate.
